Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025. We keep it short, honest, and human-readable.

1. Who we are

Humanizor ("we," "our," or "us") operates the website at humanizor.com. We provide a free AI-powered text humanizer and paraphrasing tool.

2. What data we collect

Text you submit

When you use the Humanizor tool, the text you paste is transmitted securely to our server and then to the AI provider (Anthropic) solely to generate a response. We do not store, log, or retain the text you submit. It is processed transiently and discarded immediately after your result is returned.

Usage data

We may collect anonymous, aggregated usage data (such as the number of requests per day) for operational and performance monitoring purposes. This data contains no personally identifiable information and is never linked to you as an individual.

Cookies and advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on this site. Google AdSense may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ad Settings. We do not have access to or control of the cookies set by Google.

Contact form

If you contact us via our contact page, we will collect your name and email address solely to respond to your message. We will not use these for marketing or share them with third parties.

3. How we use your data

  • To process your text and return a rewritten result
  • To display relevant advertisements via Google AdSense
  • To respond to messages you send us
  • To monitor and improve the performance of our service

We do not sell, rent, or trade any data with any third parties for commercial purposes.

4. Third-party services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Anthropic (Claude API) — processes your text to generate rewrites. Their privacy policy is available at anthropic.com/privacy.
  • Google AdSense — serves advertisements. Their privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Google Fonts — loads web fonts. This may involve your browser making a request to Google's servers.

5. Data security

All data transmitted between your browser and our server is encrypted via HTTPS. Text submissions are not written to any database or persistent storage system.

6. Children's privacy

Humanizor is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information through our site, please contact us immediately.

7. Your rights

Since we do not store personally identifiable information linked to your usage, there is generally no personal data to access, correct, or delete. If you contacted us and would like us to delete your email and name from our correspondence records, please contact us and we will do so promptly.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our contact page.

Plain English summary We don't store your text. We don't sell your data. We show Google ads. We'll delete any email you sent us if you ask. That's it.
